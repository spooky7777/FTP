# Full Throttle Performance brand guide

## Existing visual direction

- Primary red: `#D90429`
- Light neutral: `#F5F5F5`
- Heading and body text: `#333333`
- Typeface: locally hosted Roboto, weights 400, 700, and 900
- Existing identity: red, black, white, automotive imagery, bold headings, and high-contrast calls to action

## Personality

- Skilled and performance-focused
- Direct, energetic, and trustworthy
- Enthusiast-friendly without exaggeration
- Precise about workmanship, fitment, and customer expectations

## Design guardrails

- Preserve the red, black, and light-neutral identity unless a redesign is explicitly requested.
- Let genuine vehicles and shop work carry the visual impact; avoid excessive effects competing with them.
- Maintain strong contrast, visible keyboard focus, and readable body text.
- Use responsive spacing and type rather than fixed desktop-only dimensions.
- Do not stretch, crop poorly, or publish unnecessarily large vehicle images.
- Keep action labels consistent and descriptive.
