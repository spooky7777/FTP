# Full Throttle Performance site brief

## Purpose

Present Full Throttle Performance as a credible Indianapolis performance shop and turn vehicle-owner interest into calls, emails, quote requests, or scheduled service conversations.

## Primary audience

- Indianapolis-area vehicle owners seeking performance upgrades
- Enthusiasts interested in power, handling, braking, exhaust, tuning, or engine work
- Prospects who need confidence in the shop's workmanship and recommendations

## Primary actions

1. Contact the shop for a quote or recommendation.
2. Review available services and example work.
3. Find verified contact, location, and hours information.

## Current routes

- Home
- About
- Services
- Contact
- Blog templates and starter posts

## Technical profile

- Eleventy with Nunjucks templates
- LESS with generated CSS under `src/assets/css/`
- JavaScript bundled through esbuild
- Static output in `public/`
- Netlify deployment
- Decap CMS through Netlify Git Gateway

## Content rules

- Use confident, knowledgeable language without unsupported horsepower, warranty, or performance claims.
- Explain who each service is for and the outcome a customer can expect.
- Prefer genuine shop, team, vehicle, and completed-work imagery.
- Keep calls to action concrete: call, email, request a quote, or schedule a consultation.
- Do not publish prices, guarantees, policies, or supported vehicle claims until the client confirms them.

## Open decisions

- Confirm the production domain and all business contact details.
- Confirm business hours, service area, supported vehicle types, and warranty language.
- Decide whether the blog and Decap CMS are part of the active site.
- Confirm which portfolio images and customer vehicles may be published.
- Decide whether the site needs a contact form or should intentionally use phone and email only.
